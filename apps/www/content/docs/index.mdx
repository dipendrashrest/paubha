## Open Code

The top layer of every Paubha component is open for modification. When you add
a component to your project you receive the full source — not a minified
bundle, not a black box. Read it, understand it, and adapt it to your product
without any workarounds.

## Composition

Every component uses a common, composable interface. Primitives combine
predictably, props are consistent across the system, and every layout decision
is intentional. You compose features — you don't fight the framework.

## Distribution

A flat-file schema and command-line tool make it trivially easy to distribute
components across projects and teams. Publish once; consume anywhere — with
full control over versioning and change management.

## Beautiful Defaults

Carefully chosen typography, spacing, color semantics, and motion defaults mean
you get great-looking interfaces out of the box. Defaults are a starting
point, not a constraint — override any token or variant without losing
coherence across the rest of the system.

## AI-Compatible

Paubha is designed to be read and understood by large language models. The
open-code approach means AI tools can inspect, reason about, and generate
components that are immediately compatible with your existing design system —
no special plugins or integrations required.

A `skill.md` file is available to download from our landing page. Drop it into
your AI tool's context window and it will understand Paubha's conventions well
enough to generate new components and layouts that fit your product perfectly
from day one.

## Staying up to date

Teams that maintain a dedicated design-system branch find this pattern simple:
merge upstream changes into the branch, resolve conflicts with full visibility,
then promote to your main codebase on your own schedule.

## Deploy your app

Paubha works with any deployment target. Export to React, Vue, Svelte, or use
our Figma-native JSX format to hand off pixel-perfect designs. The same
semantic token layer drives both design and code, so there is never a gap
between what the designer specified and what the engineer ships.
